      Learning: spike-timing-dependent plasticity

      Synapses change strength depending on spike timing. If the presynaptic neuron fires just before the postsynaptic one, the connection strengthens; if it fires just after, the connection weakens. Move the timing difference and see the change in synaptic weight.

      About

      Clinical training, a first publication, and a change of direction toward the brain.

      I am a sixth-year medical student at Petre Shotadze Tbilisi Medical Academy in Georgia. My first publication was a microbiology study of antibiotic resistance patterns in Staphylococcus aureus, where I did the laboratory work, the statistics and the data visualization.

      That taught me how to run a study from bench to manuscript. My interest has since moved to computational and systems neuroscience, and to neuropsychiatry in particular: how signals from the brain, such as EEG and imaging, can support more objective diagnosis of psychiatric conditions.

      The research radar and the lab on this page are built to be useful, not decorative: the radar tracks new work from Swiss labs as it is published, and the lab explains models I study, in English and in Georgian, where good neuroscience material is scarce.

      Independent project

      ENIGMA–ADHD cortical thickness reanalysis

      Reproduced case-control cortical thickness effect sizes across 34 regions from published ENIGMA-ADHD summary statistics (Hoogman et al., 2019). Re-applied Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction, compared adult versus adolescent cohorts, and built interactive visualizations. Python (pandas, statsmodels, Plotly), built with AI coding assistance.
